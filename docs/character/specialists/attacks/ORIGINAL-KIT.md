`kit.json` provides explicit drawing exposures, key drawing directions, root steps, exact hit
geometry, persistent anchor identities, lead cloth, transitions and layered FX/rim/sound/camera
for N3/N4/N5, Procession/Aspersion, Nave and Te Deum. Sanctuary and Illumination are event

N2's low sweep winds into N3's upright middle-shaft baton grip. N3 gets three different accents:
small wrist-led ring, a grip-slide ring, then a toe-raised six-tick coil before the largest release.

catches the raised weapon, makes the vertical chop, loads the planted haft and vaults over it.
The upside-down hang reads before the heel-led drop and landing compression. The fixed bite and
spikes remain where they were created as the body advances. Rigid grip, weapon floor clearance
and real cloth collision are the high-risk native keys here.

N4's extraction becomes N5's yank. The14tick coil holds a wound full-body shape while a separate
faceless glass saint fills behind her. A single unwind drawing and three one-tick smears release

she stays calm. Each saint drawing differs from the hero's pose. The saint's own shatter becomes
the spike hit; it does not add a hidden independent hit. Ring/spikes survive recovery cancels.
No first-build pane absorption, Cadence or separate Pax system is added.

## Temporal and geometric limits to route

The original rev2 boxes are retained. N4f9's initial narrow blade precedes its2.5H fissure atf10;
this does not satisfy a strict instantaneous2.2H floor on that first tick. Integrator must choose
whether to add hit-matched width or preserve that intended lead-in; this specialist cannot revise
shared MOVESET. N3/N5/Q rounded prose H sizes also differ slightly from exact H96 box ratios:
export uses exact endpoint ratios and outward raster bounds. It never labels a cosmetic trail,
mini-rose or N5 saint assembly as a hitting volume.

Known hit reactions use explicit H96px/tick units, scaled by targetH/96. Known heavy flags are
carried; unspecified reactions/flags are null with an unresolved status. The final R launch
stores[3,-5]sourcepx/tick until197. Every hit declares its anchor identity: currentroot,
inputorigin, captured ringcentre, terrainward, ballistic landing, worldoculus or logicalviewport.
An unresolved viewport/oculus/landing volume never falls back silently to currentroot.

Deferred runtime requirements are persistent world anchors and terrain queries, victim-only
hitstop, stage/total freeze clocks, stored knockback, world-space light anchors, model/socket
binding, physical mesh cloth, and actual effect/audio assets. They are named dependencies,
not blockers to authoring this packet. Integrator alone resolves shared builder/runtime changes.
