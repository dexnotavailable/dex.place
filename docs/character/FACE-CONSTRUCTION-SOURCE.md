## Actual source roles

- `rosace_v2/head.py` imports the permitted source head, fills eye/mouth sockets
  flush, welds the neck, splits `head_skin` and `body`, and applies their shared
  welded normals. The visible head carries surface/contour; it does not render
  modeled eyes and mouth.
- `head.face_flattener()` uses ellipsoid normals, strength0.85, and a0.55 blend
  toward a frontal normal. These are source coefficients, not measured causes
  of the actual face gap. Its neck gate preserves the welded seam. The stored
  original welded-normal property is deleted after installation; a late hook
  must not assume that property remains available.
- `head.feature_objects()` keeps eyes/white/highlight/brow/lid/lash/mouth meshes
  hidden and weighted to the head. They are placement references, not final
  eye assets. Turning them visible would change construction/coverage and
  require a new reviewed experiment; it is not an accepted fix.
- `finish_f1/f1_blender.facepass()` projects those reference meshes and actual
  `head_skin` nose/chin vertices through the active posed camera. It records
  native head axes and per-side eye/white feature positions at sprite scale.
- `drive9/d9_post.py` cleans head skin, places authored face stamps from those
  references, then paints fringe/far-cheek/brows and softens blush. Its skin
  ramp lift and later global requantization can couple face changes to body
  pixels. Preserve exact controls and report that coupling explicitly.
