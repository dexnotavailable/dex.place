# Character critique parameters

Every review of the player character judges these parts separately. An "overall" score on its
own is never enough. Each critic compares ours against a matched reference at the same pixel
size, with left/right order randomised, and answers for each part: **ours / ref / tie**, the
concrete gap, and the fix.

The character passes when every part is a tie or better across the critic panel, and overall
at least half the critics prefer ours or can't choose. The final bar: a player of 3D gacha
games (Wuthering Waves, Genshin, Star Rail, ZZZ, Endfield) sees her and would consider
donating.

## Look

1. **First impression.** Reads at a glance at game size. Feels premium and intentional, like a
   character with a personality, not a sprite.
2. **Face.** Eye shape, size and placement; iris tones and highlights; lashes; brows; small
   nose hint; mouth; blush; chin and cheek at three-quarter view; stays consistent across
   frames; hair frames it.
3. **Genuine attractiveness.** Is she actually cute or beautiful? No uncanny, wonky or
   off-model frames. Confident, appealing expression.
4. **Head and hair.** Head-to-body ratio; hair volume; clean clumps with a clear flow; sheen;
   tapered tips; accessories.
5. **Body and proportions.** Head count; leg length; waist-to-hip and bust shape (hourglass);
   shoulders, neck, hips, thighs; stylisation consistent across the body.
6. **Arms and hands.** Arm length; elbow and wrist taper; hand size; hand shapes that read at
   pixel scale in each pose.
7. **Weapon.** Silhouette; size against her body; design ties into the outfit (gold, cross
   motifs); metal reads as metal; reads at game size; how she carries it at rest.
8. **Grip.** Where each hand sits on the shaft; one hand vs two; grip changes through a swing;
   wrists bend believably; the weapon never floats.
9. **Posture.** Line of action; spine curve; weight over the feet; contrapposto; shoulder and
   hip tilt.
10. **Posing.** Clear silhouette with negative space between limbs and body; strong key poses;
    S and C curves; attitude; variety between moves.
11. **Stylisation and exaggeration.** Poses pushed past realistic; squash and stretch; smears;
    overshoot; deep anticipation; reads as anime action, not stiff or realistic.
12. **Sex appeal, in theme.** The outfit shows shoulders, thighs, chest window and back as
    designed; poses and motion show off the figure (hip sway, hair toss); tasteful, never
    vulgar; still clearly a priest.
13. **Outfit.** Collar and cross, bodice, tabards, detached sleeves, garters, thigh-highs, boots
    all read; gold trim is clear at pixel scale; sleeves and tabards flare in motion; nothing
    changes between frames that shouldn't.
14. **Materials and shading.** Skin, white cloth with hue-shifted shadows (never flat white),
    gold, hair, stockings; band count; form lighting; real cast shadows.
15. **Palette and value.** Harmony; dark/mid/light structure; accent colours used sparingly;
    stands out from red/black/white enemies and from the background.
16. **Pixel craft.** Outline colour and selective outline; line consistency; no jaggies,
    orphan pixels or banding; clean clusters; dithering only where it helps.
17. **Rim light.** Present and readable; reacts to effect and world lights; adds form rather
    than flattening it.
18. **Originality.** Her own character, not a copy of any reference; has a memorable signature.

## Motion

19. **Idle.** Breathing; hair and cloth moving gently; personality.
20. **Run.** Contact, passing, up and down positions; lean; arm swing; speed reads; hair and
    cloth lag; turning around.
21. **Jump, double jump, land.** Anticipation, apex, fall; a flourish on the double jump;
    landing squash and recovery.
22. **Dash.** Stretch; afterimage; direction reads; clean recovery.
23. **Attacks.** Anticipation; strike speed; impact frame and hitstop; follow-through;
    recovery; how cancels feel; the weapon tip moves in clean arcs; good spacing and varied
    timing; feet plant instead of sliding.
24. **Secondary motion.** Hair, sleeves, tabards, charms lag, overlap and settle without
    jitter.
25. **Consistency in motion.** Volumes, colours and size hold from frame to frame; no pops.

## Effects and feel

26. **VFX width and grandeur.** AOE is genuinely wide; the shapes feel grand.
27. **VFX craft.** Shape language; layers (core flash, main arc, slivers, particles, ground
    decal, glow); colour ramps; timing in and out; dissipation; reads as effect, not noise;
    pixel size matches the sprite; each move has its own signature.
28. **Impact and game feel.** Hitstop; screen shake; enemy hit reaction; sound sync; input
    responsiveness; control.
29. **Camera and presentation.** Framing; zoom punches; slow motion; the ultimate's cut-in.
30. **Readability in play.** At actual game zoom, on the map, against the background, with
    enemy projectiles on screen.

## Verdict

31. **Donation test.** Would a 3D gacha player screenshot this, share it, consider donating?
    Say why or why not in one or two sentences.
