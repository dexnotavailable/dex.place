# World references

Working refs for the new world look. The images themselves are other artists' work, so they live
in the git-ignored `review/refs/world/` on Dex's PC and are never committed; this index is the
committed record of what each one is for. Refs set mood, scale, light and composition. We never
trace or copy them.

| File | What it shows | What we take | Could become |
|---|---|---|---|
| `w01-colossus-plain.png` | a pale colossal creature striding through haze over a dark plain; no people | scale without a human in frame; a creature so big it reads as weather; pale forms dissolving into haze; dark foreground ground | a loopable background: a colossus crossing the far distance while you walk |
| `w02-monolith-planet.png` | a black angular megastructure leaning into the sky against a huge pale planet | a hard dark silhouette against a bright disc; small lit windows as the only warmth; diagonal thrust | the spire where the boss arena sits |
| `w03-ring-over-lake.png` | a tilted ring megastructure over a lake, a shaft of light, one tiny person on a dock | the arrival composition: one small figure, still water, a light shaft, the structure cropped by the frame | the arrival scene |
| `w04-amber-foundry-hollow.png` | an industrial city inside a vast hollow, amber haze, cranes, ships with headlights | warmth and life inside something enormous; stacked silhouettes; light sources as points in haze | the inhabited hub (services, archive) |

## Shared rules these refs suggest

- One huge thing per scene, partly cropped, with haze between it and the viewer.
- Value does the work: dark near ground, mid-value haze, a bright disc or shaft behind.
- Tiny light points (windows, headlights, sparks) are the only fine detail in the distance.
- Motion is slow and large (a colossus, clouds, a drifting ship), with rare small flashes as accents.
