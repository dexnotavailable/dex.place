## Premise candidate: "the Procession"

Colossi walk an endless line around the edge of the world, toward a broken ring that hangs tilted
over the sea. They don't notice anyone. People live in the hollows of fallen megastructures and
keep lights burning at shrines along the colossi's route, so travellers can find their way.

Rosace is one of the lamplighters: a priestess who tends the shrines and fights the wardens that
guard the old spire. Nobody explains the ring, the colossi or the spire. The story is small and
human: lights tended, a route walked, a city that keeps going under enormous things.

## Route

1. **Arrival: the dock under the ring** (w03). One small figure, still water, a shaft of light
   through the ring. Nothing to do; scenery first. Left: the end of the pier, a place to sit
   (rest). Right: the shore path rises.
2. **The plain** (w01). A long misty causeway. A colossus crosses far behind you, slowly, on a
   loop; it never notices you. The first shrine: a rest place, a donation box, and the map banner
   (slash its cord and it unrolls; E to read it).
3. **The hollow** (w04). Down into the amber city inside a fallen structure: warm, lived-in, sparse.
   The registry counter (dex account), donation boxes, and the terminal hall. Deep inside, behind
   an ordinary door, the **archive** (documentation), quiet.
4. **The spire** (w02). A lift up the black spire against the pale planet. At the top, the
   **arena**: use the terminal to summon a warden, win, cut to black, choose a download. Every
   visit is a new fight.
5. **The pilgrim path to the gallery** (optional, longer). Back out toward the ring; climb a
   fallen ring segment to a **chapel of light**, where Dex's art hangs, one piece at a time. At
   the end, a view from above: the ring, the sea, the colossi walking in a line on the horizon.
   A shortcut drops you back at the dock.

Shrines (rest places) sit between areas; each has a donation box and its top-donor record.
