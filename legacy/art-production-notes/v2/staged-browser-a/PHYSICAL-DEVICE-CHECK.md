# Physical device review readiness

Run against the root-confirmed final review URL after its candidate is available to the device. Loopback `127.0.0.1:5190` is workstation-only and is not a phone/iPad URL. Record the release ID first; candidate A's results do not automatically certify B or the eventual public deployment.

Record device model, OS version, Samsung Internet or iPad Safari version, orientation and network. Use both portrait and landscape. A short recording is more useful than an isolated screenshot for control/scroll/audio transitions.

1. Fresh entry: loading image matches the first world; choose Sound on/off, Enter once and immediately move. Test simultaneous move/jump/attack, double jump and Dash. Change touch size/handedness, rotate, open/close Menu and resume. No stuck held movement or cropped controls.
2. Walk a doorway/loading transition, cut the map and inspect it, inspect one artwork and close. Scroll into the website and back; inputs stop while reading and resume deliberately. Full pages and the keyboard remain scrollable without pulling the game unexpectedly.
3. Download the latest documentation and choose1.0 once. Confirm browser download UI, not installation. Open a documentation deep link, search, and return. Inspect art at Fit/zoom and use Previous/Next/Escape-equivalent Back.
4. MB Bank: inspect100,000 and10,000,000VND, generate/save the QR, optionally import the saved image in the banking app **without confirming a payment**. Record the resolved recipient/account/amount and whether camera scan or image import was used. The earlier100k recipient confirmation remains valid only at its recorded scope.
5. Optional voice review requires the root-confirmed live service and another admitted participant on a separate network. Explicitly enable Nearby/voice, allow microphone only through the real permission prompt, hold/release the mobile talk control, rotate/blur/close reader and verify capture stops. Test mute/block and distance with recognizable participants. Do not treat a synthetic one-machine tone as this proof.

Actual screen-reader speech remains a separate coordinated check. No new accounts, test donations, password changes or microphone recording are required merely to perform the ordinary browser review.
