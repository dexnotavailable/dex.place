# Material and scale pass — local room renderer

The current scene has60 source-role definitions and116 separately placed images, plus stateful mechanisms/actors. All11 rooms rendered without missing textures/errors in the latest capture. `receipt.json` binds their map/room/scene/game-source hashes. This is a visual checkpoint, not public delivery or native-traversal proof. The separate integrated App tours own native travel and original-art inspection.

## What changed after looking at the actual scene

- Hearth had a blank wall, tiny counter and an unmotivated ceiling light. Separate plaster/wainscot, timber spans, bookcase and fireplace now enclose the counter. The original60-frame CC0 fire is its own sprite, at uniform2x scale, with a separately cropped foreground grate/body over it. Room lighting is warm and local; exterior fog no longer fills this interior.
- The lookout's new stone landmark initially appeared suspended above the path. It now extends below the water plane; opaque atmospheric palette compression places it behind the solid foreground trestle. The huge opening and cropped mass dominate the small character. Source and toned variant remain separately preserved.
- Oversized ceiling textures made their pixels chunkier than the rest of the gallery. Smaller repeated native spans replace the stretched full-room image. Gallery light wells now use raster frames and actual wall openings instead of plain luminous rectangles.
- Dispatch/Arena's architectural art was hidden behind the procedural wall. Its major rib/recess layers now sit in front of that wall. Empty outlined placeholder bays were removed. The arena's open floor and broad shadowed arch give the fighter a clear silhouette and room to move.
- Archive has separately placed bookcases behind the small physical files. Treasury retains the large arch and restrained service light. Their quieter volume contrasts with the warm refuge and the open sea route.
- Benches use the actual bench source, rather than a spare lift-deck image. Door leaves retain their native scale and fixed aperture. Gallery frames are aspect-aware native nine-slice assemblies, so wide works no longer occupy a narrow band in a tall frame. Personal art remains live DOM display only; it is deliberately absent from these neutral renderer plates.

## Inspection boundaries

These captures use explicit local lookdev commands. The camera/character is placed at the room centre on a supporting surface where available. They do not prove walking, fighting, lift timing or a release. The first failed capture used two different Vite Phaser module identities; the helper now resolves the actual dependency URL and the latest captures pass. That failure receipt is retained.

The root inspected full-size Hearth, Lookout, Gallery, Archive and Arena frames and the complete room contact sheet. Current composition is suitable to carry into the frozen App review; final desktop/phone/iPad presentation, original-art display, audio and all service flows remain separate requirements. New UI/game feedback changes after this checkpoint require the final renderer-source/preview seal to be refreshed.

## Loading frames

`../capture-previews-v11.mjs` is prepared to capture actual authored arrival/door anchors at six display classes: compact landscape, phone portrait, tablet portrait, tablet landscape, desktop and ultrawide. This avoids using one wide image across every viewport. It excludes personal art and records world rectangles, anchors, room bounds and source hashes. The v11 frames are not yet captured or installed; no claim of first-frame acceptance is made here.
