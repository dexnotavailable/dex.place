## Implemented owner surfaces

- `WorldScene.ts`: private-world simulation, active-room bounds, door transition, movement/double jump/full sword follow-through, stable checkpoint recovery, product-encounter lifecycle, per-art intents and pose publication.

- `progress.ts`: bounded semantic-ID discoveries/cuts/shortcuts/checkpoint/history, no raw position or transient fight/menu restoration. Restored arena checkpoints resolve safely at the product approach. Root/services own persistence and account/guest merge.
- `cast.ts`, `population.ts`: native authored Wizard2, rat, bat and townsfolk; actor clip pivots/timings; sparse mob pockets, damage/knockback/death; boss attack/recovery/hit/death ownership. Bat fall/death uses the separately verified clip pivots.
- `releasedMap.ts`: slash-only physical fastening release, falling cable, native cloth/weight settling, permanent cut state and later E inspection. Closing an inspector does not furl it. The cut is recorded at contact and restores as settled, never midway through motion.
- `doorway.ts`: separately materialed frame/leaf with an opening state; target entry is validated before transfer. Missing target room material fails at the source rather than entering incomplete art.
- `ghosts.ts`: bounded tinted remote pose rendering, no local collisions/combat/mechanism effects, geometry/dynamic-support filtering, membership withdrawal. Root/services own authenticated membership/activity/voice.
- `contracts.ts`: additive typed progress/pose/ghost and room/service/product intents/commands; legacy command consumers remain compilable.

Environment/Lighting were not edited by this lane. Root's Environment.setRoom is called before presenting the new room. PUBLIC_ASSETS now names the actual v2 map, graph, scene and cast manifests; downstream staging/audio/source guards must use these rather than the first-pass causeway files.

## Exact parent/service seams

- New intents: room-change, room-transition, progress, pose, map-inspect, service, product-approach, product-menu and health. Existing navigate/projection/effect/combat events remain.
- New commands: enter-room, enter-encounter, leave-product-visit, restore-progress, request-progress, ghosts and inspect-map. A product encounter only starts for the currently selected valid product at Dispatch. Victory never requests an artifact.
- Door opening/ready uses a gameplay-owned half-second transition clock. Ordinary pause/blur stops it; root's transition presentation must not itself pause that clock. A missing material/destination emits failed with the source/target/door identity.
- Pose sends at10Hz. Meaningful movement requires input plus actual displacement, not interpolation/camera jitter. Room geometry comes from the exported allowlist. Bridge support requires settled local/remote state; lift support rejects locked state and compares current dock/moving-Y state. Peer sprites never enter activeSolids or Population.
- Every map/bridge cut is local and stable; restored mechanisms start settled/docked. Gallery return-lift release persists independently of current lift position. Menus, held keys, moving clocks, live health/fights and visit tokens are never saved.
