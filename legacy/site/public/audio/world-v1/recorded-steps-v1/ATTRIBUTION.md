# Recorded footsteps v1

Unapproved integrated comparison. The four quiet WAV derivatives retain the existing concrete/metal effect IDs.

Concrete source: **Kenney, Impact Sounds 1.0**, `footstep_concrete_001.ogg` and `footstep_concrete_003.ogg`. [Official source](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds). License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original pack license retained in private production provenance. Stereo channels averaged arithmetically; fixed −21 dB attenuation for both clips. No per-hit normalization.

Metal source: **SoftDistortionFX, Metal Footsteps**, actual hard-soled walking on a metal grate. [Official sound page](https://freesound.org/people/SoftDistortionFX/sounds/398937/). License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). These derivatives use the public HQ MP3 preview, not the original FLAC: ranges6.14–6.50s and12.42–12.78s, fixed −9dB attenuation for both,5ms cut-edge fades. No per-hit normalization.

Export: mono PCM16 WAV at48kHz. Sources, processing ranges and SHA-256 identities are recorded in the audio manifest. Source rights and successful decoding do not constitute artistic/listening approval.
