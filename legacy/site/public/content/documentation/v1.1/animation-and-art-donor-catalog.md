# Animation and art donor catalog

Source specification 2.1 research · Verified 2026-09-08 · **Adopted intake reference; implementation active, with current inventory/proof in19/26.**

This is a curated intake queue for chapter 21's inhabited nonlinear world, not permission to download everything or a claim that the packs already fit. Creator pages, explicit free-file lists and licence statements were inspected. Four creator preview images/GIFs were viewed in an isolated browser. No asset archive, font or source sheet was downloaded, installed, edited, rehosted or added to the public site. Native archive completeness, dimensions, pivots, frame timing and runtime appearance remain unverified until intake after go. Paid additions are excluded.

The existing LuizMelo Martial Hero remains the player. Its inspected local manifest uses 200×200 frame cells, a foot pivot at(100,122), approximately 52 px visible idle height and a 22×46 collision guide. The transparent cell is not the body's scale. Compare donors beside that actual actor at native size before choosing world scale; a 32 px NPC and a 250 px sheet cell do not become compatible merely because both are pixel art. The character's authored frames stay coherent; never regenerate isolated poses to manufacture a sprite sheet.

<a id="first-intake-order"></a>
## First intake order

1. **Boss:** Evil Wizard 2 first, with Evil Wizard 3 as the closer human-scale alternative. Keep only the one whose actual swing/contact/death reads best in the arena. Fire Knight is a stronger alternate moveset if its proportions and credit requirement work.
2. **Sparse enemies:** Monsters Creatures Fantasy 2 rat/bat or one original-pack creature. Choose one ground and one air silhouette at most for the first connected route; a four-creature archive is not a demand to use all four.
3. **Residents:** Gothicvania Town's side-view townsfolk first. The free Blacksmith is an excellent service-motion reference but a conditional actor because its32px proportions are squat. The49-NPC pack supplies idle personalities, not a finished walking/service cast.
4. **Environment and detail:** retain the original dex.place architecture. Test selective Magic Cliffs depth and one small fire/water detail; do not replace the world with a donor demo.
5. **UI:** compare one Kenney frame family with m5x7 or Monogram for short labels. Keep Daniel for `dex`, and the readable body/financial-text system for articles, accounts and banking.

All placements, palette adaptation and animation timings below are proposed production choices. The art/use restrictions in 01/03/21 remain unchanged; none of Dex's illustrations is a donor or generation reference.

<a id="gaps-the-catalog-does-not-pretend-to-solve"></a>
## Gaps the catalog does not pretend to solve

No inspected free pack here proves a complete side-view archive door with handle/latch/open/close frames, a cable-cut/falling/settled map, or a resident handing over an account service object at the current world's exact scale. Those remain authored asset/state work after go. Existing dex.place mechanism parts can supply aligned structure; this catalogue supplies compatible actors/material/motion leads, not ready-made game behavior. Doors must preserve fixed pivots, entrance/readiness/collision and return anchors. Map wind/cloth loops cannot replace its one-time cut and persistent open state.

Water motion is covered only as a conditional splash/foam/drip source and existing calm world-water material; no ocean-view page is treated as proof of animated water. Hearth work/greeting is covered by a small service donor, but a natural-height side-view attendant may still need an authored gesture adaptation. Record these missing roles in 19 rather than marking them acquired because a similarly named pack exists.

<a id="mandatory-intake-and-scene-test-after-go"></a>
## Mandatory intake and scene test after go

1. Acquire only the chosen free archive from its creator. Preserve its exact original, version/date, file hash and included licence; compare free/paid filenames against this dated page evidence. Fonts, audio, demo code and third-party credits remain separate components.
2. Enumerate every clip and frame; record actual cell dimensions, opaque bounds, foot baseline, pivot, loop/hold behavior and required external props/projectiles. Check all stated actions are really present across any update archives. Page counts are discovery evidence, not importer truth.
3. Build one native-size lineup beside the untouched Martial Hero, P02 terminal and current platform. Compare body proportion, pixel pitch, palette, facing and side-view perspective at desktop and phone camera sizes. Reject a weak donor instead of stretching it until the nominal height matches.
4. Exercise complete animations: idle→walk→stop, greeting/work→service panel→return, anticipation→blade/projectile contact→follow-through, hit interruption and full death→black→product menu. Wire collision events independently of animation render rate. No compressed preview GIF becomes the source sheet.
5. Inspect fixed mounts versus moving pieces for fire/door/cloth/map/lift. Match light direction and source size; preserve transparent edges. Use Low/reduced modes without eliminating useful states, and avoid converting every calm room into an effects reel.
6. Test UI9-slicing, native glyph rendering, hover/focus/pressed/disabled/open/close and every long/Vietnamese/account/bank string. Select one pixel heading/label family; body text, accessible names and QR remain ordinary readable DOM.
7. Record licensing/attribution in the private asset register and future reviewed credits. [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits reuse/adaptation without attribution requirement; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) requires appropriate credit, licence link and indication of changes. Custom restrictions are not interchangeable with either. Preserve the final project attribution even when credit is optional; it is factual documentation, not promotional copy.
8. Publish only the reviewed runtime derivatives needed by the site after the new-go workflow and final verification. Never include donor archives, paid extras or font/source bundles in the site's documentation download. Recheck expanded image/audio/network budgets and actual frame timing; the old 51-role proof cannot certify this larger set.

This catalog proposes intake order and replacements; it does not change 21/22's adopted scope, SunoB selection, existing public release, published documentation 1.0, accounts, or hosting.
