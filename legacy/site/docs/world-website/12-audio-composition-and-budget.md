Explicit Mute instead sets master gain to zero and stops output immediately, with no 200 ms delay.

## Loop construction and transitions

1. Preserve the provider's original native file and record its codec, actual duration, BPM/key assessment, source revision and generation metadata. When the source is lossy, a decoded WAV is an editing master, not a restored lossless original.
2. Choose musically compatible boundaries from the accepted arrangement, preserving reverb tails through offline overlap rather than abrupt clipping. The old48-second/80 BPM rule is superseded by20's56 BPM authored source and Suno arrangement review. Requested generator duration is not a measured bar grid or output length; do not force a selected phrase into the old timing target.
3. Remove clicks at the loop seam. Preview at least three complete repeats, headphones and speakers; verify perceived rhythm, harmony, noise-floor change and tail continuity. A generator's “loop” prompt is not proof.
4. Ambience loops use an offline 1–2 second overlap if needed, with no distinct recurring bird/click/whoosh revealing the seam. Check that overlap does not create a volume swell or flanging.
5. Export runtime files and decode them through the actual browser route. Encode padding and actual decode duration matter; verify the final file, not just the editor timeline.

Current section transitions retain the shared theme's transport between audible regions. Entering a quiet region fades the outgoing voice over200 ms before stopping it and retaining its offset; returning to an audible region uses a500 ms music fade-in. The region's zero gain must not collapse the outgoing fade early. Rapid navigation cancels obsolete work. Silence is represented by no source, not a downloaded silent file. The older one-bar/equal-power transition at80 BPM was a proposed five-cue design; it is not implemented or required for this single-theme direction. Any future multi-cue transition needs its own verified phase, musical compatibility and loop treatment rather than inheriting that historical three-second timing.

On rapid navigation, the newest desired cue wins. Cancel stale loads/fades by audio revision; at most the current and incoming music cues can play. Once the fade finishes, stop/disconnect the old source. Visiting the same section/item does not restart the cue. Audio never delays DOM navigation or moves the camera.

Audio state priority: explicit mute or hidden page > explicit pause/menu > active content panel > active encounter > world region. Content-specific silence is intentional; an ordinary panel's simulation pause must not accidentally replace its room's sound policy with global Menu pause.

Use one audio manager and one realtime playback context for the app, plus the single lazy48 kHz decode-only owner described below. Repeated component mounts, route changes or StrictMode checks must not create duplicate music players. Buses: master, music, effects and ambience. Existing SYS controls own opt-in, mute and gain; no new track picker or audio permission flow is added.

## Formats, levels and resource budget

- Private authoring: provider-native originals,48 kHz WAV editing/mix masters, note/render sources, edit decisions and provenance. A converted WAV is not proof the provider supplied lossless audio. Preserve separately downloaded provider WAV and MP3 representations with their actual lengths and tails. The current runtime accepts48 kHz source metadata and verifies decoded rate/channels; other source rates require a documented route change, not implicit hardware-rate decoding.
- Runtime music: stereo Opus around96 kbps where decoding is supported, with AAC-LC around128 kbps as an alternate. Choose one compatible source; do not download/decode both formats. Required compatibility is proven on the actual target browsers.
- Runtime ambience: mono compatible compressed audio. Short effects: trimmed mono PCM/WAV where small enough or compatible compressed alternatives. Avoid a large dependency solely for an audio player.
- Decode through one lazy `OfflineAudioContext(2, 1, 48000)`, never invoking offline rendering. The realtime context stays at the output device's rate and resamples at playback. Keep the64 MiB decoded-cache cap, reserve using actual source-rate metadata and encoded-padding allowance, and count every returned buffer as `length * numberOfChannels * 4`. Release the decoder reference on disposal. Unsupported metadata or a missing decoder produces the existing recoverable Sound error; it does not fall back to unbounded hardware-rate decoding. The historical two48-second-cue/20-effect estimate is not the current selected-media budget.
- Evict unused cues/effects before decoding; stop/release an outgoing cue before a replacement when overlap would exceed the cap. Never trim or damage a musical phrase merely to fit this cache. If a selected complete master needs streaming, implement and verify that route separately. Until a required source fits and decodes, Sound remains off with its readable error while the DOM site/game remains usable. Browser-internal decoder scratch/GPU/process memory is not included in the measured application buffer total.
- At most two music sources overlap, two ambience sources, and 12 simultaneous SFX voices. Duplicate hits and UI actions coalesce according to their event IDs; quiet low-priority sounds may drop at the voice limit. Never drop the visual gameplay cue.
- Music/ambience fetch begins only after opt-in. Fetch the current cue first; prefetch at most the likely next cue when network/memory conditions allow. Hidden/unused cues do not preload the whole soundtrack.
- Aim for a restrained mix around-22 to-18 LUFS for finished music, with headroom and true peaks no higher than-1 dBTP. These are proposed mix targets, not a guarantee that a provider output arrives normalized. Audition effects at real game volume; slider percentages alone cannot establish loudness.
- Default gain percentages remain in [01](01-art-direction-and-audio.md). Keep meaningful attacks readable at low listening volume and all gameplay understandable with sound off.
