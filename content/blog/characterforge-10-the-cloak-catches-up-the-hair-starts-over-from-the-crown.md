Six new renders since update 09. The headline is the cloak: in a blind test against professional game models it landed inside the judges' own noise, which is the first time a piece of clothing has done that. The skin-tight outfit is still clearly behind, so it is being rebuilt from a single traced garment mask. The face kit dropped the pale eye ring and the crude open mouth the judges kept naming. The hair got the most attention: round 17 rebuilds it from one point on the crown, smooth on top and separating toward the tips, and it is the best-scoring hair so far, but it stays unpublished until it clearly beats the current version. We also froze everything as a baseline with step-by-step recipes, so any of these results can be rebuilt exactly. Work pauses here until the weekly usage reset.

Only our own renders appear here. No concept art, no other studios' models or stills, and the AI-made ghost mesh is never shown without a tracing-guide label.
